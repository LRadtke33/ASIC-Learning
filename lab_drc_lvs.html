Lab 7 — DRC and LVS Debug (Magic, KLayout, Netgen)

Take a SKY130 HD inverter, prove a clean row, inject a metal1 spacing error and two LVS faults, then read the same checks on the clean SPM from Lab 6. Antenna is lesson 51, not a second copy.

Recorded evidence. Magic 8.3.623, KLayout with sky130A_mr.drc, and Netgen with sky130A_setup.tcl were run on 11 November 2026, America/Chicago, in the LibreLane nix environment, on the layouts in labs/L07_drc_lvs/. Reports are in labs/L07_drc_lvs/results/. The SPM signoff reports are from LibreLane run RUN_2026-11-11_16-09-06, copied under labs/analysis_examples/rtl_to_gds/. Antenna ratios and diode insertion are not re-done here; they are lesson 51.

What you will do

  • See why an isolated standard cell fails nwell and latch-up DRC, and why a tap–inv–tap row does not.
  • Inject a 0.05 µm metal1 gap and read met1.2 / m1.2 in both DRC engines.
  • Extract SPICE with Magic and compare it to the library inv_1 in Netgen: a unique match, a short, and a swapped pin.
  • Point at the same checks, already clean, in the Lab 6 SPM run.

1. Sources and tools

The lab folder labs/L07_drc_lvs/ contains:

  • row_clean/ — tap_1 + inv_1 + tap_1 on unithd sites, GDS_FILE properties stripped so Magic uses the painted .mag.
  • row_spacing/ — the same row plus a metal1 rectangle 0.50 × 0.20 µm, 0.05 µm below the VPWR rail (the m1.2 limit is 0.14 µm; the rectangle’s area is above the 0.083 µm² m1.6 minimum).
  • clean/, lvs_short/, lvs_pinswap/ — the inverter alone, for extraction: a locali short from A to Y, and the A/Y labels swapped.
  • ref/inv_1_lib.spice — the library subcircuit (nfet 0.65/0.15, pfet_hvt 1.0/0.15).
  • run_magic_drc.tcl, run_extract.tcl, run_gds.tcl — the batch scripts that were run.

Set PDK_ROOT (and PDKPATH) to your sky130A installation. Invoke Magic with the PDK rcfile, non-interactively:

export MAG=$PWD/row_clean/inv_row.mag
export RPT=$PWD/results/drc_row_clean.rpt
magic -noconsole -dnull -rcfile "$PDK_ROOT/sky130A/libs.tech/magic/sky130A.magicrc" \
    run_magic_drc.tcl

KLayout, after Magic writes a GDS:

klayout -b -zz -r "$PDK_ROOT/sky130A/libs.tech/klayout/drc/sky130A_mr.drc" \
    -rd input=results/row_clean.gds -rd topcell=inv_row \
    -rd report=results/row_clean_klayout.lyrdb \
    -rd feol=1 -rd beol=1 -rd floating_metal=0 -rd offgrid=1 -rd seal=0

Netgen, after Magic extraction:

netgen -batch lvs \
    "results/extract_clean.spice sky130_fd_sc_hd__inv_1" \
    "ref/inv_1_lib.spice sky130_fd_sc_hd__inv_1" \
    "$PDK_ROOT/sky130A/libs.tech/netgen/sky130A_setup.tcl" \
    results/lvs_clean.rpt

2. Recorded DRC results

An isolated inv_1 (no taps) is not a fair “clean” baseline. Magic reported five errors: nwell.4 (nwell without a metal-connected N+ tap), LU.2 and LU.3 (diffusion-to-tap < 15 µm). Abutting tap_1 on both sides, on unithd sites, cleared them.

LayoutMagic 8.3.623KLayout (sky130A_mr.drc)
Isolated inv_1COUNT: 5 — nwell.4, LU.2, LU.3(not used as the clean baseline)
tap–inv–tap, no extra metalCOUNT: 00 items
tap–inv–tap + 0.05 µm m1 gapCOUNT: 4, all “Metal1 spacing < 0.14um (met1.2)”, boxes at (0.86, 2.34)–(1.36, 2.43) µm and along the rail at y = 2.48–2.57 µm1 item, category m1.2, edge-pair (0.86,2.43;1.36,2.43)/(1.491,2.48;0.729,2.48)

The marker figure and the reason two engines can disagree on count while agreeing on the rule are in lesson 62, Figure 2. Reports: results/drc_row_clean.rpt, drc_row_spacing.rpt, row_clean_klayout.lyrdb, row_spacing_klayout.lyrdb.

3. Recorded LVS results

Magic extraction of the clean cell produced:

.subckt sky130_fd_sc_hd__inv_1 A VGND VNB VPB VPWR Y
X0 Y A VGND VNB sky130_fd_pr__nfet_01v8 ... w=0.65 l=0.15
X1 Y A VPWR VPB sky130_fd_pr__pfet_01v8_hvt ... w=1 l=0.15
.ends

Netgen compared that with the library subcircuit.

CaseWhat was doneNetgen final result (recorded)
CleanUnmodified inv_1.mag2 devices, 6 nets both sides. Pin lists equivalent (A, Y, VGND, VNB, VPB, VPWR). Final result: Circuits match uniquely.
ShortA locali rectangle from A to Y. Extracted subckt dropped pin A; both transistor gates sit on Y. Nets 5 vs 6, Mismatch.Netlists do not match. Final result: Top level cell failed pin matching.
Pin swapThe painted geometry is unchanged; the labels A and Y are exchanged.2 devices, 6 nets, “Netlists match uniquely,” but Y | A **Mismatch** and A | Y **Mismatch**. Final result: Top level cell failed pin matching.

The pin-swap is the easy one to misread: the transistor graph is fine, the ports are not. Always read down to the “Final result” line. Full reports: results/lvs_clean.rpt, lvs_lvs_short.rpt, lvs_lvs_pinswap.rpt.

After you have seen a failure you can recognize, look at a chip that passed. The 45% SPM of Lab 6 ran both DRC engines, XOR, extraction, LVS and the manufacturability wrapper:

  • Magic DRC: [INFO] COUNT: 0 — labs/analysis_examples/rtl_to_gds/spm_45_drc.magic.rpt.
  • KLayout DRC: 257 rules, all 0 — spm_45_drc.klayout.json.
  • XOR: Total XOR differences: 0 — spm_45_xor_excerpt.txt.
  • Netgen: 328 devices, 361 nets, “Circuits match uniquely.” — spm_45_lvs.netgen.rpt.
  • Manufacturability: Antenna Passed, LVS Passed, DRC Passed — spm_45_manufacturability.rpt.
  • Antenna counts are 0; the explanation of the check is lesson 51, not a repeat of that lesson.

5. What to hand in

If you re-run the scripts: the Magic COUNT lines for the clean and spacing rows, the KLayout item count and category, and the Netgen “Final result” line for each of the three LVS cases. If your PDK revision differs, the coordinates may shift slightly; the rule IDs and the unique-match / pin-mismatch distinction should not. State the Magic, KLayout, Netgen and PDK versions.
