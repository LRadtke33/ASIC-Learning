Lesson 52 / 84 · Part VI, Physical cells and the floorplan

Decap Cells

A decap cell is a MOS capacitor between the power rails, dropped into empty sites. The channel gets longer as the cell gets wider. The Liberty file used here does not publish a femtofarad.

Where the numbers come from. Outlines are the decap LEF files. Devices and lengths are the schematic spice. The missing capacitance is decap_4 at tt_025C_1v80. The flow settings are from the open_pdks LibreLane configuration (formerly OpenLane). Lesson 47 already introduced the family.

What you will learn

  • Explain how a decap cell uses a MOS transistor as a capacitor between the rails.
  • Read the SKY130 decap family: 3 to 12 sites wide, with longer channels in wider cells.
  • Size decoupling from charge first, then check impedance and leakage.

Where this fits · the flow map is lesson 8

In the flow
step 7, routing fills empty sites with decap cells; step 8, extraction and timing checks the supply.
Tools
OpenROAD’s filler_placement places them and analyze_power_grid checks voltage drop. Commercial: Innovus, Voltus.
Why it matters
Capacitance on the die supplies fast current demands that the package cannot.

1. Empty sites can hold a capacitor instead of a spacer

A fill cell (lesson 47) continues the row and has no devices. A decap cell occupies the same kind of gap and puts a capacitor between VPWR and VGND. The capacitor is two MOSFETs used as capacitors, not a drawn metal-to-metal cap. Charge on the rails has a local reservoir when a gate switches (lesson 14, lesson 30).

The family is decap_3, decap_4, decap_6, decap_8, and decap_12. Class is CORE, not SPACER. Site is unithd. There is no decap_1 and no decap_2 in the cells checked.

Every schematic is exactly those two devices. The PMOS line is w=870000u, the NMOS line is w=550000u, and the length goes from l=590000u on decap_3 to l=4.73e+06u on decap_12. The u suffix multiplies a SPICE number by 10−6: 870000u evaluates to 0.87. In these published SKY130 subcircuit calls W and L use the wrapper's micrometer convention. Do not copy that convention onto a primitive MOS model or a differently scaled simulator deck without checking its units. Logic length in this library is 0.15 µm. These channels are 0.59 µm to 4.73 µm because the row is only 2.72 µm tall and the capacitor has to grow sideways.

The Liberty file for decap_4 at the typical corner records area 5.0048 µm², which is 1.84 × 2.72, and cell leakage 0.003240044 nW. It has power pins and no capacitance attribute. The file gives no capacitance value. A longer channel is a bigger capacitor. The femtofarads come from a device model.

3. The flow uses the family in two places

The historical OpenLane configuration selected the decap_ family and used decap_3 at row ends. The current open_pdks LibreLane configuration uses DECAP_CELLS = sky130_fd_sc_hd__decap* and ENDCAP_CELL = sky130_fd_sc_hd__decap_3. These are flow choices for this library, not proof that arbitrary decaps may serve as arbitrary libraries' endcaps. The gap must fit a legal width and respect tap, well, and rail rules.

Terminal order in the schematic matches the picture. The PMOS line is drain VPWR, gate VGND, source VPWR, body VPB. The NMOS line is drain VGND, gate VPWR, source VGND, body VNB. Both channel ends of each device sit on the same rail, so the MOSFET is a capacitor from its gate to that rail. The PMOS capacitor hangs on VPWR with its gate at ground. The NMOS capacitor hangs on VGND with its gate at power. From the rails' point of view the two are in parallel between power and ground.

decap_4 leakage, 0.003240044 nW, is close to conb_1's 0.003240037 nW and is not the same cell (lesson 50). The area 5.0048 µm² is the outline, 1.84 × 2.72, not a capacitance.

A MOS capacitor is bias- and frequency-dependent. These opposite-rail connections put the MOSFETs into inversion when the supply is high enough; source/drain contacts provide the inversion charge. Depletion, startup at low supply, finite channel resistance, and contact resistance can reduce useful capacitance or change its bandwidth. Accumulation is a different charge regime, not an inverted channel. These gates are tied to the opposite rail, so at the 1.8 V supply they are biased as capacitors. Digital readers need the placement facts: class CORE and the widths in the table. Analog readers need a capacitance, which comes from simulating the device model, not from the Liberty file.

4. The channel is the second plate

A MOS capacitor in these cells is a transistor whose gate is one plate and whose inverted channel is the other. Source and drain are tied to the rail opposite the gate, which is why the schematic shows a device from VPWR to VGND with the gate on one rail.

Plate area grows with width times length. Width is fixed in the family, 0.87 µm PMOS and 0.55 µm NMOS. Length is the column that changes, from 0.59 µm to 4.73 µm. At a fixed operating condition, greater gate area generally increases capacitance. Leakage does not follow a universal “longer means leakier” rule: mechanisms depend on area, electric field, device, temperature, and bias. The listed TT Liberty leakage barely changes across this family, so those values do not establish a useful physical leakage-versus-length law. Characterize supply current and admittance with appropriate device models.

5. Size decoupling from charge, then check impedance

Worked example, not a SKY130 cell specification: suppose a local load draws an extra 1 mA for 1 ns before upstream current responds. Holding rail droop below 20 mV requires at least C = ΔI·Δt/ΔV = 50 pF in an ideal charge-balance model. A decap count cannot be obtained from cell area alone. If characterization at the relevant bias and frequency found 5 fF of usable capacitance per candidate cell, the ideal count would be 10,000; that 5 fF is a hypothetical input, not a measured decap_4 value.

Use the capacitor close enough to the switching load that its connection impedance is useful. Check the combined die/package/board impedance; adding capacitance can shift resonances. A capacitor supplies a transient current deficit and later recharges. It cannot remove the steady IR drop of a persistently overloaded supply route.

Across the boundary: digital bursts disturb analog supplies and reference returns. Coordinate switching activity, PDN impedance, placement, and analog supply rejection. See lesson 83.

Check your understanding: if the current deficit lasts 2 ns instead of 1 ns, what changes?

The ideal capacitance doubles to 100 pF for the same 1 mA and 20 mV target. ESR and inductive droop still need separate evaluation. In a real PDN, the upstream source may supply part of that current, so integrate the actual current deficit rather than all load current.

Check your understanding — a local load needs 0.5 pC during a supply dip that may not exceed 50 mV. How much capacitance must sit nearby?

C = Q/ΔV = 0.5 pC / 50 mV = 10 pF of effective capacitance, close enough that its connection resistance and inductance do not dominate. The capacitance of the decap cells must come from the device model, because the Liberty file gives none.

Key takeaways

  • Decap cells are MOS capacitors between the rails. Fill cells are spacers with no devices.
  • decap_3/4/6/8/12 are 3, 4, 6, 8, and 12 sites. Class CORE. Height 2.72 µm.
  • Every one has a 0.87 µm high-Vt PMOS and a 0.55 µm NMOS. Length runs from 0.59 µm to 4.73 µm.
  • decap_4 Liberty has area and leakage and no capacitance. No femtofarad is stated here.
  • This HD flow selects the decap family and decap_3 as its endcap; verify the installed flow configuration.
  • Decap capacitance is a MOS gate over an inverted channel. Greater gate area adds capacitance; leakage and high-frequency usefulness need separate characterization.
