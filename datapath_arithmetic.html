Lesson 44 / 84 · Part V, Digital implementation

Datapath & Arithmetic

Full adders, majority gates and what Yosys actually maps for a 16-bit add and an 8-bit multiply on sky130_fd_sc_hd.

Where the numbers come from. Cell outlines are the sky130_fd_sc_hd LEF in this PDK. The headline mapped areas are Yosys 0.52 (fee39a3) with ABC, stat -liberty, on 11 November 2026, America/Chicago, against a copy of sky130_fd_sc_hd__tt_025C_1v80 from which LibreLane Classic’s default exclusion lists had been removed — the same two files the Lab 6 SPM resolved as SYNTH_EXCLUDED_CELL_FILE (libs.tech/openlane/sky130_fd_sc_hd/no_synth.cells, 199 names) and PNR_EXCLUDED_CELL_FILE (…/drc_exclude.cells, 53 names). Yosys applies both lists at synthesis (236 cells removed, including every lpflow_*). A comparison run on the unfiltered Liberty is in section 2, labelled as such. RTL, lists, scripts and stat reports are in labs/analysis_examples/datapath/. Delay, a prefix-adder implementation, and a Booth -booth comparison were not run and are not quoted.

What you will learn

  • Name the SKY130 HD adder cells and their LEF areas: ha, fa, fah, maj3, xor3.
  • Contrast a structural ripple-carry of fa_1 with what ABC emits for an inferred + of the same width.
  • Read a multiplier mapping that uses maj3 and xor3, and know what was not measured (delay, Booth, prefix).

Why it matters
The library already has a full adder. Whether your synthesiser uses it, and what that does to area, is a measured fact, not a slogan.

1. The cells

A half adder is SUM = A ⊕ B, COUT = A·B. A full adder adds a carry-in: SUM = A ⊕ B ⊕ CIN, COUT = majority(A,B,CIN). That majority is exactly maj3, which is why a mapper may build an adder from maj3 and xor/xnor instead of instantiating fa_1. The fah / fahcin / fahcon variants change the polarity of the carry pins so a ripple can avoid inverters on the carry chain.

2. Ripple versus inferred add, on this library

The structural netlist is sixteen instantiations of sky130_fd_sc_hd__fa_1, carry chained, CIN of bit 0 tied to the port. Yosys stat -liberty reports 16 cells and 320.3072 µm² (16 × 20.0192). The inferred RTL

assign {cout, s} = a + b + cin;

went through proc, techmap and abc -liberty on that filtered Liberty and came back as 121 cells, 1,089.7952 µm². The histogram is all ordinary HD logic at drive _2 (LibreLane’s no_synth.cells lists the _1 of many functions, so ABC cannot pick them): 18 nor2_2, 16 and2_2, 14 nand2_2, 13 or2_2, 11 xnor2_2, 8 xor2_2, and no fa_1, ha_*, maj3_* or lpflow_*. fa_1 is not on either exclusion list — ABC had it available and still did not use it. The structural ripple remains a legitimate, denser alternative whose delay this run did not measure.

Why the exclusion lists matter

LibreLane Classic 3.0.17 does not hand Yosys the raw Liberty. In librelane/steps/yosys.py it builds a temporary Liberty with toolbox.remove_cells_from_lib, taking the union of:

  • SYNTH_EXCLUDED_CELL_FILE — on this PDK, sky130A/libs.tech/openlane/sky130_fd_sc_hd/no_synth.cells (199 names): clock buffers and delay cells, scan flops, the _1 (minimum-drive) variant of many combinational cells, and the arithmetic specials ha_*, fah_* and maj3_*.
  • PNR_EXCLUDED_CELL_FILE — …/drc_exclude.cells (53 names): every lpflow_* cell (34 of them), xor3_*, xnor3_*, fa_4, and a handful of DRC-awkward or zero-drive cells. Synthesis applies this list as well as the first.

Those are the same paths written in the Lab 6 SPM’s resolved.json. Copies of both files are in labs/analysis_examples/datapath/. The union is 236 cells; this run removed 236 cell blocks from the Liberty and kept 192.

A comparison run on the unfiltered Liberty, with the same Yosys script, reproduced the earlier figures exactly: the inferred adder was 107 cells and 713.184 µm², including four maj3_1, and ABC used four lpflow_inputiso1p_1 and five lpflow_isobufsrc_1 as if they were ordinary gates. The inferred multiplier was 244 cells and 1,904.3264 µm², with 31 maj3_1, 6 xor3_1, 12 xnor3_1, and seven more lpflow cells. Isolation cells have a sleep pin and a power-domain job; they do not belong in an always-on datapath. That is why a real flow removes them before ABC sees the library. The filtered areas are larger (1,089.7952 vs 713.184 for the adder; 2,828.9632 vs 1,904.3264 for the multiplier) because the lists also take away the _1 drive and the majority/xor3 gates ABC preferred. 1,090 µm² is still not “worse” than 320 µm² without a delay measurement.

3. A multiplier, similarly inferred

An 8-bit assign p = a * b; mapped the same way, on the filtered Liberty, to 293 cells, 2,828.9632 µm², with 35 xnor2_2, 31 nand2_2, 25 a21o_2 and 9 xor2_2 — again no fa_1, and no maj3 or xor3 (those are excluded). Array, Booth and Wallace organisations are the usual textbook names for how partial products are formed and reduced; Yosys can be asked for a Booth recoding (-booth), but that switch was not used here and no area for it is quoted.

4. Prefix adders, in outline

A ripple-carry’s critical path walks every COUT — 16 full-adder carry delays, in series, for a 16-bit RCA. Carry-lookahead and prefix adders (Kogge–Stone, Brent–Kung) compute generate/propagate terms in a tree so the MSB carry is a logarithmic number of gate delays, at the cost of more gates and more wiring. They were not written or mapped for this lesson. The FO4 delay unit of lesson 29 is the right language for a later comparison; no FO4 count is invented here.

On the unfiltered Liberty, ABC used lpflow isolation cells in the adder. After LibreLane’s lists, it did not, and the area rose from 713 to 1,090 µm². Which number should you quote?

The 1,089.7952 µm² figure, from the filtered Liberty. That is what LibreLane Classic actually synthesizes with: both no_synth.cells and drc_exclude.cells, as resolved on the Lab 6 SPM. The 713.184 µm² result is a real Yosys mapping, but it is not a legal netlist for a flow that forbids those cells — isolation gates were used as ordinary logic, and minimum-drive and majority cells that the PDK’s LibreLane lists remove were used as well. Quote the filtered number; keep the unfiltered one only as the reason the lists exist.

fa_1 is not on either exclusion list, yet the inferred 16-bit add still used none. Is that a bug?

Not on this evidence. ABC is allowed to build the same truth table from xor2_2, and2_2 and friends. The structural 16 × fa_1 ripple is a legitimate, denser alternative (320.3072 µm²) whose delay this run did not measure. A bug would be a wrong truth table, which was not checked here either — only area and the cell list.
